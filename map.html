FAQs

Common Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

What industries does Reinerde serve?
Reinerde serves textile, petrochemical, oil & gas, municipal, and agro-industrial sectors. Our solutions cover wastewater treatment, oil recovery, effluent recycling, and environmental compliance across these verticals.
How quickly can your team respond to an emergency environmental situation?
For urgent situations, our field response team can be mobilised within 24–48 hours for sites within Tamil Nadu. Contact us directly by phone for fastest response.
Do you offer on-site water quality testing and analysis?
Yes. Our team provides on-site sampling, water quality analysis, and effluent characterisation as part of our project assessment process. Lab-based detailed analysis is also available through our partner facilities.
Can Reinerde help with regulatory compliance and environmental permits?
Absolutely. We assist clients with CPCB and TNPCB compliance, ETP design, zero liquid discharge (ZLD) systems, and all required documentation for environmental permits and audits.
Do you offer internship or research collaboration opportunities?
Yes! We actively collaborate with engineering colleges and research institutions. Internship applications are open — send your resume to info@reinerde.com with the subject "Internship Application".
