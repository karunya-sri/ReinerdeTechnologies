How Reinerde Bioculture Works

Each product is a precision-engineered blend of beneficial microbes that restore balance in your treatment systems — naturally.

1

Select the Right Product

Choose from our range based on your specific system — aerobic, anaerobic, composting, or septic. Each formulation is purpose-built.

2

Dose & Inoculate

Add the bioculture directly to your treatment tank, digestor, or reactor. Our microbes activate rapidly in the existing environment.

3

Restore & Sustain

MLSS builds up, COD/BOD reduces, odour disappears. Your system runs efficiently — with results visible within days of dosing.
